### ArchUnit Structural Guardrail

Rule `Web_component_code_behind_classes_should_not_directly_depend_on_infrastructure` enforces
that `Ray.BiliBiliTool.Web.Components.*` types do not take a direct dependency on
`Ray.BiliBiliTool.Infrastructure.*`. Passed: 5/5 architecture tests green.

Note: Domain types (`ExecutionLog`, `BiliLogs`) are legitimately used in component code-behind
for display binding. The rule correctly targets `Infrastructure` only.
